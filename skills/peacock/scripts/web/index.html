🦚 Peacock — how to read the map

  • Two views, press v (or the 🌌 / 🗺 buttons). Nebula is the 3D graph — beautiful, but long edges fade into the distance and depth is ambiguous. Atlas is a flat map: every directory is a labelled rectangle, its files a grid inside, and every arc visibly starts and ends.
  • In Atlas, drag to pan and scroll to zoom (no orbiting — that is the point). Containment needs no lines: the rectangle is the containment. Cross-directory imports collapse into one weighted route per pair, and click a file to fan out its functions and see its exact wires.
  • Every point is a code entity. Colour = type (see the legend), size = importance (connections).
  • Lines are relationships — containment, imports and calls.
  • Orbit by dragging, zoom by scrolling, pan with right-drag or two-finger drag.
  • Hover to name a node, click to inspect it and highlight its neighbourhood.
  • Search to fly the camera to a symbol.
  • Extend live (right panel) to graft a new import/library on and watch it settle in.
  • Open in VS Code — with a node selected, press o (or use the Inspector button) to jump to that file/function in your editor. Peacock offers to open the project on launch.
